Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Kyrazo (“we”, “our”, “us”) collects, uses, and protects personal data when you use our website and services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum personal data required to provide account access and operate the service.

1.1 Account Information

  • First name & Last name
  • Email address
  • Job title (optional)
  • Profile avatar

1.2 Authentication Data

  • Securely hashed passwords
  • Last login timestamp
  • Email verification status

1.3 Third-Party Authentication (Google)

If you sign in using Google, we receive basic profile information provided by Google (Name, Email, Image). We do not receive access to your Google password or private data.

2. Payment Information

All payments and subscription transactions are processed securely by Stripe. We do not store or process credit card or payment details on our servers.

3. How We Use Personal Data

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Authenticate users and secure access.
  • Process subscriptions and payments.
  • Communicate essential service-related messages.
  • Maintain security and prevent unauthorized access.

We do NOT sell personal data or use it for advertising purposes.

4. Legal Basis (GDPR)

If you are in the EEA, we process data based on contractual necessity, legitimate interests (security), user consent, and legal obligations.

5. Data Sharing

We share personal data only with trusted service providers: Stripe (payments), Google (auth), and our infrastructure providers.
