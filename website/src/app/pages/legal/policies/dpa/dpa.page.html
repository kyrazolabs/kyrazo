Data Processing Agreement

Last Updated: January 1, 2026

1. Introduction

This Data Processing Agreement (“DPA”) forms part of the Terms of Service between Kyrazo LLC (“Processor”) and the customer using the Kyrazo platform (“Controller”). This DPA applies to the processing of Personal Data subject to Data Protection Laws (including GDPR and PDPL) in the course of providing our high-performance webhook relay services.

2. Processing of Personal Data

Processor shall only process Personal Data for the following purposes:

  • Providing, maintaining, and improving the Services.
  • Processing in accordance with Controller’s documented instructions.
  • Ensuring the performance, security, and integrity of the Service infrastructure.

3. Technical & Organizational Measures

Processor implements industry-standard measures to protect Personal Data, including:

  • Encryption: Payload encryption using AES-256-GCM (at rest) and TLS 1.3 (in transit).
  • Data Residency: Primary processing and storage occur within the EEA (Netcup Germany/Austria).
  • Backups: Securely encrypted snapshots stored within the EEA.
  • Isolation: Logical separation of data between different Controller workspaces.
  • Retention: Data is automatically deleted in accordance with the Controller's active subscription plan for all ingest and dispatch events.

4. Sub-processing

We use a limited number of third-party sub-processors to providing our services. A current list and their processing locations can be found on our GDPR Page.

5. Data Subject Rights & Audits

Processor shall assist Controller in responding to Data Subject requests and provide reasonable information necessary to demonstrate compliance with these data protection obligations.

6. Jurisdiction

This DPA is governed by the laws of the United States, without prejudice to the mandatory protections afforded to Data Subjects under the GDPR or PDPL where applicable.
