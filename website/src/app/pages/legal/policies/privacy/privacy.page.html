Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Kyrazo LLC (“we”, “our”, “us”) collects, uses, and protects personal data when you use our website and services. We are committed to processing your data securely and transparently.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the minimum personal data required to provide account access and operate our high-scale webhook infrastructure.

1.1 Account Information

  • Name & Email address
  • Workspace configurations
  • Authentication metadata

1.2 Event Data

  • Webhook payloads (Headers & Bodies)
  • Delivery status & attempts
  • Endpoint URLs

2. Data Security & Encryption

We implement application-level encryption for all sensitive data. Payloads moving through our system are protected using AES-256-GCM encryption.

  • In Transit: All data is encrypted using TLS 1.2+ (HTTPS).
  • At Rest: Sensitive fields and high-volume event payloads are encrypted before being stored in our databases.

3. Data Residency & Hosting

Our infrastructure is designed for performance and compliance:

  • Primary Storage & Hosting: All application and event data is processed and stored on Netcup infrastructure in Germany/Austria (EU).
  • Backups: Encrypted snapshots are replicated to EEA (EU) regions for disaster recovery via Cloudflare R2.

4. Data Retention

We adhere to the principle of storage limitation. Data is purged based on your service agreement and subscription tier:

  • Ingest & Dispatch Events: Deleted automatically based on the **customer subscription** terms.
  • Processed Webhooks: Retained according to subscription-level retention settings.
  • Account Data: Retained for the duration of your active subscription.
