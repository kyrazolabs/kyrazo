Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2026

This Refund & Cancellation Policy applies to all subscriptions and payments made for Kyrazo.

1. Subscription Cancellation

You may cancel your subscription at any time through your account settings or by contacting us.

  • Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period.
  • You will continue to have access to paid features until the end of that period.
  • No further charges will be made after cancellation.

2. Refunds

Unless otherwise stated, all fees are non-refundable.

We do not provide refunds for:

  • Partial billing periods.
  • Unused time.
  • Downgrades.
  • Cancellations made after a billing cycle has started.

3. Exceptions

Refunds may be issued at our sole discretion in exceptional cases, such as duplicate charges or billing errors. Any approved refund will be processed through the original payment method via Stripe.

4. Payment Processor

All payments and refunds are handled securely by Stripe. Processing times may vary depending on your bank or payment provider.

5. Contact

If you believe a charge was made in error or have questions regarding billing, contact us at:

billing@kyrazo.com
