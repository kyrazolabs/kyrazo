GDPR Compliance

Effective Date: January 1, 2026

1. Commitment to GDPR

At Kyrazo, we are committed to ensuring the security and protection of the personal information that we process. Our infrastructure is built with "privacy by design," ensuring that data protection principles are integrated into our core event-driven architecture.

2. Data subject Rights

We facilitate your rights under the GDPR, including:

  • Right of Access: Request a copy of your personal data processed by us.
  • Right to Rectification: Correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to Erasure: Request deletion of your data (subject to retention policies).
  • Right to Portability: Receive your data in a machine-readable format.

3. Technical & Organizational Measures

Our backend implements rigorous security standards to protect your data:

  • Encryption at Rest: Sensitive payloads (Headers & Bodies) are encrypted using AES-256-GCM.
  • Encryption in Transit: All API traffic is secured via TLS 1.3 or higher.
  • Tenant Isolation: Strict isolation of workspace data to prevent cross-tenant access.
  • Access Control: Secure credential hashing and role-based access control.

4. Sub-processors

We utilize carefully selected sub-processors to maintain our high-performance infrastructure:

Netcup (Germany/Austria)

Primary cloud infrastructure and hosting within the EEA.

Stripe (USA/Global)

Payment processing and billing management.

Cloudflare (Global/EEA)

DNS, WAF, and secure object storage backups.

PostHog EU

Usage analytics hosted within the EEA for product optimization.
