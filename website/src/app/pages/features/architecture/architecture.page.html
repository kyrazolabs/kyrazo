The Dedicated Workers
Architecture

Why do standard webhook services fail at scale? Because of shared resources. Kyrazo gives you isolated compute lanes for your events.

Physical Isolation

We don't use multi-tenant "logical" sandboxes. We use physical isolation. Your worker is yours. A spike in traffic from another customer simply cannot affect your throughput.

Predictable Latency

Shared queues suffer from "head-of-line blocking". With dedicated workers, your P99 latency remains consistent even during Black Friday levels of global platform traffic.

Security Boundaries

Isolation isn't just about speed; it's about security. Your payloads are processed in memory spaces that are logically separated from other tenants.
