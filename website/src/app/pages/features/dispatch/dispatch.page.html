Smart Retries

Configure exponential or linear backoff strategies. Define max attempts and retry intervals per destination. We handle the jitter and timing.

Dead Letter Queues

If an event fails after all retries, it's moved to a DLQ. You get an alert, and can manually inspect and replay it when your service is back online.

Scheduled Delivery

Send events in the future. Perfect for trial expiration emails, follow-up sequences, or delaying processing to off-peak hours.
